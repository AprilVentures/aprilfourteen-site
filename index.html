02 · Approach

How an app gets made.

Every app goes through the same four stages, and we don't skip any of them.

  1. STAGE 01

    Listen

    We find a job shops are doing by hand, then ask a lot of owners how they handle it today. What they tell us decides what gets built.

  2. STAGE 02

    Build

    One job per app, built to feel like part of Shopify. No bundles, and no features added just to fill a pricing page.

  3. STAGE 03

    Test

    We run it on real test stores, start to finish, before a shop ever sees it. Then it goes through Shopify's own review.

  4. STAGE 04

    Launch, and stay

    We email everyone who asked to hear about it. After that we keep fixing, improving and answering.

PROMISE 01

Plain words

Your customers read what our apps put in front of them, so it's written the way you'd say it across the counter. No jargon and no small print doing the heavy lifting.

PROMISE 02

Straight answers

Every app's site says what it needs and what it doesn't do. If it isn't right for your shop, we'd rather tell you now than after you've set it up.

PROMISE 03

Built on Shopify

Our apps work with Shopify's own checkout and admin wherever they can, so your orders and your customers stay where you already look for them.

PROMISE 04

Real people, not a queue

Questions go straight to the people who built the app. No scripts and no ticket numbers, just a proper answer.

03 · About

Who's behind it.

Olly here, I'm the founder of Aprilfourteen.

It started with a simple question to shop owners: how do you do it today? Again and again the answer was a job that really mattered to the business, held together with a spreadsheet, a notebook by the till or somebody's memory.

Shopify does a huge amount, but it doesn't do everything. Those gaps are where we work. We pick one, talk to as many shops as will have us, and build the app we'd want if it were our own shop.

We're a small studio on purpose. It means you get straight answers, quick fixes and people who know the app inside out. If there's a job like that in your shop, I'd love to hear about it!

Oliver Barry, founder of Aprilfourteen
